# The Mine Cart Explorer docking into a scaled-up Wagon (concepts)

Aaron, 26 Nov 13:13: "read the specs on the wagon and consider its designs. You will find that it has a station for a rolling cart in the back. I want to generate some concepts of this mine cart scout docking into the wagon. scale the wagon model up to the size that could store the minecart scout in the rear. This may take a few passes to join the models in a good concept. begin"

- **Scale:** the approved Wagon (round 64) is about 19 x 7.1 x 6.6 m; her back box (vehicle bay) is 4.95 m long, 5.4 m wide, 3.0 m clear, with a 3.0 x 2.9 m ramp opening (Driftwright `modules/wagon/README.md`). The Mine Cart Explorer is about 7.4 x 4.4 x 3.8 m (dish folded). Scaled up 1.65x, the Wagon is about 31 x 11.7 x 10.9 m, her bay about 8.2 x 8.9 x 5 m, the ramp opening about 5 x 4.8 m.
- References: the Wagon's approved port side and back (uploaded by Aaron, `upload:ZVcSbdiJEQwKkDT9je6t`, `upload:N4ZmUPW55P9IsJbmmjzj`) and the Explorer's locked views (`xHzJeIywLczCrPeNXoJY`).
